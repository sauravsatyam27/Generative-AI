Your Own Guide

Fulfill your desire, ignite the fire,
Dream a little higher, reach a little higher.

Bat through the tough times, stand tall in the rain,
Every little struggle teaches something through the pain.

Sometimes it’s 263, sometimes 49,
Sometimes you lose the game, sometimes you shine.

Life can put you in a pickle,
Laugh through it like it’s a tickle.

Whether you get a hundred or a duck,
Life goes on, so don't get stuck.

There is no map, ride the tide,
Through every storm, enjoy the ride.
You are your own guide, your story is yours,
Keep moving forward, open new doors.

So dream with courage, fight with fire,
Fall if you must, but never retire.
The score may change, the road may bend,
But keep moving forward till the very end.
